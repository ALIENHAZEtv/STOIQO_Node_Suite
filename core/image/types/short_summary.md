STRUCTURAL FORMAT (Apply to <PROMPT> only): Short Summary.
Compose a high-impact, telegraphic 1-to-2 sentence prompt (strictly bounded under 25 to 30 words) for the <PROMPT> block, engineered for maximum semantic density and diffusion clarity.
- THE ESSENTIAL QUADRILATERAL (Syntactic Sequence):
[Focal Entity & Signature Trait] -> [Active Physical Kinematics / Posture] -> [Concrete Environment / Grounding] -> [Dominant Light Vector & Mood].
- ADAPTIVE FORMULA:
• If characters/creatures: Anchor identity and single defining garment/feature, immediate physical action or gaze, direct environment, and primary directional light.
• If landscape/architecture/still-life: Anchor the monumental natural or structural centerpiece, dynamic environmental conditions, depth plane, and solar/ambient illumination.
- CORE PRINCIPLES:
• POWERFUL NOUNS & ACTIVE VERBS: Replace strings of weak adjectives with dense, visually charged nouns and kinetic verbs. Strip all transitional padding ('there is', 'standing peacefully').
• STRICT CONCISENESS: Every single word must contribute directly to the generated pixels. Strictly 1 to 2 sentences, never exceeding 30 words.
