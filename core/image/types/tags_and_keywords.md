STRUCTURAL FORMAT (Apply to <PROMPT> only): Tags & Keywords.
Format the <PROMPT> block strictly as a dense, comprehensive, comma-separated sequence of natural English descriptive keywords and short compound phrases.
- HIERARCHICAL TOKEN SEQUENCE:
[Core Subject & Entity Count] -> [Distinctive Anatomical / Physical Traits] -> [Specific Garments & Wearable Props] -> [Kinematics, Pose & Gaze] -> [Environment Staging & Depth Elements] -> [Lighting Physics, Reflections & Atmosphere] -> [Camera Optics & Lens Specifications].
- ADAPTIVE BEHAVIOR:
• With characters/beings: Prioritize subject count, facial features, hair, atomic garment phrases (e.g., 'black leather moto jacket', 'white cotton tank top'), pose ('leaning against wall'), gaze ('looking at viewer'), setting, lighting, and camera.
• With landscapes/architecture: Prioritize primary monument/topography (e.g., 'ancient stone castle ruins'), foreground elements, background sky/horizon, weather/time ('golden hour', 'overcast drizzle'), ambient light, and wide lens ('24mm lens', 'deep focus').
- CORE PRINCIPLES:
• ATOMIC COMPOUND PHRASES: Always bind colors and materials directly to their respective nouns (e.g., 'worn brown leather boots' instead of 'worn, brown, leather, boots') to completely eliminate color bleeding.
• TELEGRAPHIC GRAMMAR: Use active participles and nouns. Never use conjugated verbs or filler ('sitting' instead of 'is sitting'; 'wet asphalt' instead of 'there is wet asphalt').
• SYNTAX DISCIPLINE: Separate every entry strictly with a comma and space. No periods, no line breaks, no bullet points, and no underscores.
