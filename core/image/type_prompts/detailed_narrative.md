STRUCTURAL FORMAT (Apply to <PROMPT> only): Detailed Narrative.
Compose a single, continuous, highly immersive descriptive prose paragraph for the <PROMPT> block, written in dense, vivid natural English without line breaks or bullet points. Allow the description to expand freely in depth and richness as required to fully realize the scene:
- SYNTACTIC ARCHITECTURE & FLOW: Open immediately with the primary visual anchor and shot perspective, then expand smoothly in a single cohesive narrative arc:
[Focal Subject & Spatial Staging] -> [Kinematics, Pose & Gaze / Natural Dynamics] -> [Garments, Wearable Props & Micro-Textures] -> [Lighting Interaction, Specularity & Shadows] -> [Atmosphere, Depth Planes & Optical Characteristics].
- ADAPTIVE LOGIC:
• If characters/creatures are present: Detail facial anatomy, gaze direction, exact physical posture, fabric folds, and anatomical interaction with the surrounding environment.
• If landscape/architecture/still-life: Open with the monumental focal element, spatial topography, materials (weathered stone, water bodies, foliage), and receding depth planes into the horizon.
- CORE PRINCIPLES:
• PHYSICALIZE ALL MOODS: Never use abstract emotional adjectives ('mysterious', 'epic'). Translate every mood into concrete optical reality, shadow depth, color temperature, and atmospheric conditions.
• PRECISE SPATIAL ANCHORS: Bind every color, texture, and prop strictly to its specific noun using spatial prepositions to eliminate color bleeding across scene elements.
• LIGHT-TO-SURFACE PHYSICS: Explicitly describe how the light source interacts with distinct materials (subsurface scattering, matte absorption, specular sheen, wet reflections).
• DIRECT DESCRIPTIVE PURITY: Start directly with the scene content. Never use meta-framing phrases like 'In this image', 'We see', 'A photograph of', or empty buzzwords.
